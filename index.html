01

Research Interests

Human-demonstration data collection

Datasets from diverse setups such as teleoperation and wearable gloves that go beyond image–action pairs to include object pose, contact points, and contact forces as observations, which are hard to collect in the real world yet essential for more advanced robot learning.

Hierarchical planning and control

A high-level planner such as a VLM produces plans that diverse low-level controllers (VLA, RL, or classical controllers) execute, solving long-horizon, contact-rich tasks beyond the reach of a single policy and scaling to new tasks and robots.

Research Experience

Daegu Gyeongbuk Institute of Science and Technology (DGIST)

Undergraduate Research Intern, ROBIN Lab

Jul. 2026 – Sep. 2026Daegu, Republic of Korea

  • VICON (Visual–Inertial–Contact based Hand–Object Tracking): Developed a multimodal human-demonstration framework that jointly captures hand motion, object pose, contact locations, and normal-force measurements using a wearable glove and a single RGB-D camera, enabling contact-rich data collection without a motion-capture setup.
    • Occlusion-Robust Object Tracking: designed an offline factor-graph refinement that combines visibility-aware object-pose estimates with contact-conditioned hand-motion cues, maintaining trajectories through severe hand occlusion and reducing tracking failures from 50.9% (FoundationPose) to 2.5%.
    • Contact & Force Annotation: reconstructed metric object meshes from monocular video and mapped synchronized glove force measurements onto the object surface to recover object-frame contact points and calibrated normal forces.
    • Robot-Learning Data Pipeline: built an end-to-end recording and annotation pipeline for synchronized RGB-D, hand–object trajectories, contact, and force signals, designed for force-aware manipulation and VLA training.

Korea Institute of Industrial Technology (KITECH)

Student Researcher, National Engineering R&D Center

Jan. 2026 – Jun. 2026Ansan, Republic of Korea

  • Skill-wise Expert based Data Generation: To reduce the high cost of teleoperation data collection, generated VLA fine-tuning data by simulator rollouts with skill-wise experts (teleop demos → Diffusion Policy based BC → Residual RL).
  • VIVA (VLM-Instructed VLA Architecture): To overcome the long-horizon limitation of a single VLA model, designed a hierarchical VLM-VLA architecture where Qwen3-VL decomposes commands into skill plans and asynchronously updates π0.5’s instructions based on real-time scene understanding.
